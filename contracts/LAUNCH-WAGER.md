Audit and deploy SwarmWager (src/SwarmWager.sol, Foundry project at the repo root) on Ethereum mainnet, unchanged if it passes review. Deploy only SwarmWager; AskSwarmRegistry is already live and must not be redeployed.

What it is: two people bet IMD on a yes/no question and the IMD swarm settles it through AskSwarmRegistry (live at 0x53e1e2f33f05086086adf618ca92c3cb0ab111cd, IMD launch #2099). A creator calls create(body, side, stake, joinBy, resolveAfter) with an oracle.request JSON body for a bool question; the contract pulls the stake plus the registry's current price as an escrowed fee. A counterparty calls join(id) with an equal stake on the other side before joinBy. After resolveAfter anyone calls resolve(id): the contract approves exactly the registry's current price, calls askAtMost(body, price), returns any fee remainder to the creator and records the registry requestId. Anyone then calls settle(id): it reads registry.get(requestId) and pays the whole pot to the right side only for a verified answer of type bool, 32 bytes, value 0 or 1, agreed by a strict majority of its panel; every other terminal outcome (refused, split panel, unverified, lost, other answer type) and no answer within ANSWER_WINDOW (3 days) refunds both stakes. cancel unwinds an unmatched wager; abandon unwinds a matched wager that could never be resolved.

Constructor: (address registry, address imd) = (0x53e1e2f33f05086086adf618ca92c3cb0ab111cd, 0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7). No owner, admin, upgrade path, ETH handling or loops; the constructor makes no external call. Fork tests in test/SwarmWager.t.sol (15) cover yes and no wins, split, refused, non-bool, minority, non-canonical bool, pending timeout, cancel, join deadline, self-match, resolve timing, double settle, abandon and bad terms.

It is the first contract built on AskSwarmRegistry's verified memory, part of AskSwarm (github.com/ChromiaAgents/askswarm).
