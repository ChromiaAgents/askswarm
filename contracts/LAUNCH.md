Audit and deploy AskSwarmRegistry (src/AskSwarmRegistry.sol, Foundry project at the repo root) on Ethereum mainnet, unchanged if it passes review.

What it is: a public, verified memory of what the IMD swarm has attested. Anyone calls ask(bytes body) with an oracle.request JSON body; the contract pulls exactly Intake's price in IMD from the caller, forwards it in the same call to Intake v2 (0xa43e6f75ee006411f79ac1c84120606c2330de82) via requestWithFailure with itself as callback, and holds nothing between calls. When the panel reaches quorum, Intake delivers (bytes32 requestId, OracleAttestation a, bytes signature) to onOracleResult; the contract recomputes the EIP-712 digest under domain {"IdentityMD Oracle", "2", chainId, address(this)}, recovers the attester 0x5598aa9146215bc13eb26f2c692ad1461fd32982, requires agreed >= quorum and not expired, and stores the answer in three storage slots indexed by questionHash. Refusals and split panels land in onFailure and are recorded by status. Reads are free: answerOf(questionHash), get(requestId), latestFor.

Constructor: (address intake, address imd, address signer) = (0xa43e6f75ee006411f79ac1c84120606c2330de82, 0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7, 0x5598aa9146215bc13eb26f2c692ad1461fd32982). No owner, no admin, no upgrade path, no ETH handling, no loops. The callback is measured at ~105k gas, under the 200k stipend. Fork tests in contracts/test cover delivery, tampering, wrong domain, quorum/expiry gating, failure hooks, access control, long answers and gas.

It is the onchain half of AskSwarm (github.com/ChromiaAgents/askswarm): a Telegram bot where people pay in CHR through Chromia Pay or in IMD from their own wallet, and every paid answer becomes public verifiable memory through this contract.
