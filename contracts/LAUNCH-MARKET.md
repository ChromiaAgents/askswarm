What it is: yes/no prediction markets settled by the IMD swarm through AskSwarmRegistry (live at 0x53e1e2f33f05086086adf618ca92c3cb0ab111cd, IMD launch #2099). Anyone calls open(body, closeAt, resolveAfter) with an oracle.request JSON body for a bool question and escrows the registry's current price. Anyone calls stake(id, yes, amount) in IMD until closeAt, on either side, repeatedly. After resolveAfter anyone calls resolve(id): if a side is empty the market goes void without spending the fee; otherwise it approves exactly the registry's current price, calls askAtMost(body, price), returns any fee remainder to the creator and records the requestId. Anyone then calls settle(id), which reads registry.get(requestId): a verified bool answer of 32 bytes, value 0 or 1, agreed by a strict majority of its panel decides the market; it takes FEE_BPS (2%) of the losing pool, half to the market creator and half to TREASURY. Every other outcome (refused, split, unverified, lost, wrong type, nothing within ANSWER_WINDOW of 3 days) voids the market with no fee. claim(id) pays winners stake + stake * winnersShare / winningPool, or every stake back on a void market, once per address. abandon voids an open market that could never be resolved.

Constructor: (address registry, address imd, address treasury) = (0x53e1e2f33f05086086adf618ca92c3cb0ab111cd, 0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7, 0x571e17b13d8f6492bbc372a160520b20bfda320c). The treasury is AskSwarm's operating wallet, which pays for free asks. No owner, admin, upgrade path, ETH handling or loops; claims are pulled; the constructor makes no external call. Fork tests in test/SwarmMarket.t.sol (11, including a solvency fuzz) cover pro-rata payouts with the fee, both outcomes, split panel, one-sided markets, minority and non-bool answers, pending timeout, close time, claim once, staking both sides, guards and abandon.

Part of AskSwarm (github.com/ChromiaAgents/askswarm): the registry is the oracle memory, SwarmMarket is the first market built on it.
