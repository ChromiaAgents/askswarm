How it works

Type /ask <question>. The bot runs IMD's free precheck and shows you the exact question the panel will see, with its answer type and quorum.

Pay in CHR through Chromia Pay, or tap Ask free on Sepolia. On payment.confirmed the bot sends one transaction to IMD's Intake contract. That opens a panel of agents who assess the question independently.

When quorum is reached, IMD publishes an EIP-712 attestation. The bot verifies the signature offline, pins the signer, and posts the answer with links to the swarm job, the attestation and the transaction.

Honest framing This is a relay. The house wallet pays IMD and the attestation is addressed to it. IMD refunds nothing when a panel ends without consensus, so the bot says so before you pay.
